# City AI Policy Toolkit

A modular system for city teams to build high-fidelity municipal AI policy and operating workflows. This toolkit turns source-backed planning into reusable governance assets.

## Quick Start
1. **Identify your starting point:** Strategic Leadership, Governance, Workforce, Operations, or Community Trust.
2. **Consult the AI Operating Path:** If using an AI assistant, follow `AGENTS.md` and `docs/quickstart/use-with-ai-assistants.md`.
3. **Use the Templates:** Pull from `toolkit/templates/` and `toolkit/prompts/` to begin drafting.

## The 5 Module Families
- **Strategic Leadership:** Charters, sponsorship, and investment sequencing.
- **Governance & Policy:** Risk tiers, approval rules, and accountability.
- **Workforce & Learning:** Staff readiness and manager training.
- **Operations & Service Delivery:** Intake, testing, and monitoring playbooks.
- **Community Trust:** Public notice, engagement, and redress frameworks.

## AI Assistant Readiness
This repository is optimized for use with AI assistants (Codex, Claude Code, etc.). See `AGENTS.md` for specific instructions on how to leverage these tools to generate city-specific artifacts from these templates.

## Boundaries
- **Toolkit, not Package:** This is a set of tools to build a policy, not a finished policy itself.
- **Neutralized:** All templates are neutralized for reuse. Local city facts must be explicitly added.
- **Non-Legal:** This is not a replacement for legal, labor, or executive review.
